01 · What I found

The opportunity is not more credibility. It is clearer translation.

David already has a real platform, a defined Michigan audience, recurring content, partnership offers, and meaningful reach. The profile makes those ingredients harder to connect into one immediate answer: who is this for, and what does David help them accomplish?

01

Lead with the buyer

Make Michigan business owners and community-minded partners visible in the first line, not after a list of capabilities.

02

Turn proof into context

The profile cites strong reach, organic distribution, and newsletter engagement. The next step is connecting those numbers to what they mean for a prospective partner.

03

Make the profile match the platform

The business is increasingly a local visibility platform, not simply a personal storytelling practice. LinkedIn should reflect that evolution.

02 · Current positioning

What the profile says today

Current identity
Founder of Michigan Small Business Spotlight, with a mix of local-business storytelling, community partnerships, social media, and visibility support.
What is still unclear

Why is Michigan Small Business Spotlight different from ordinary local advertising or social-media support?
03 · Headline

Your current headline

As shown on the supplied profile
Founder, Michigan Small Business Spotlight | Local Business Storytelling, Community Partnerships & Social Media | Helping Michigan Businesses Get Seen
Why it can be stronger

It contains the right ingredients, but it asks the reader to assemble the story. A referral, post, or search visitor should not have to decide whether David is primarily a media founder, a social-media provider, a community partner, or a storyteller. The strongest direction makes the audience and outcome unmistakable, then uses the business name as proof of what sits behind the promise.

Five directions

Choose the one that feels most like you

I help Michigan small businesses get seen, trusted & remembered | Founder, Michigan Small Business Spotlight

Leads with the audience and outcome, then anchors the claim to the business.

Option 2

Founder, Michigan Small Business Spotlight | Telling the stories that help Michigan businesses get discovered

Keeps the media identity central while making the purpose instantly clear.

Option 3

Michigan Small Business Storytelling | Community Partnerships, Social Content & Local Visibility

Strongest for partnerships and organizations that care about community visibility.

Option 4

Helping Michigan business owners turn their stories into local visibility | Founder, Michigan Small Business Spotlight

Connects David’s storytelling work directly to the visibility problem he solves.

Option 5

Founder, Michigan Small Business Spotlight | Stories, Social & Community Partnerships for Local Businesses

A balanced option that preserves the current service mix without feeling crowded.

What is working
Specific audience and service categories are already present.
The profile contains unusually useful proof: views, organic reach, Facebook reach, and newsletter engagement.
The mission, helping local businesses get seen, trusted, and remembered, is memorable and ownable.
What needs tightening
The strongest value proposition arrives after several descriptions.
The different offers can feel like separate services instead of one coherent visibility platform.
The reader needs a faster path from "what David does" to "why it matters to me."

Your new About

I run Michigan Small Business Spotlight, a Michigan-focused media platform built to help independent businesses get seen, trusted, and remembered. Every week, I find the owners and community builders worth knowing and turn their stories into written features, social content, interviews, video, and newsletter coverage. The goal is simple: make good local businesses easier for Michigan residents to discover and remember. The platform has already built meaningful proof of attention. In the first 7.5 months, our content reached more than 1.25 million views across our platforms, with more than 97% organic reach. I also work with businesses and community-minded organizations that want local visibility without generic marketing. That includes Spotlight Club partnerships, done-for-you Facebook and Instagram support, dedicated business-story interviews, and a permission-based Customer Return System pilot for qualifying repeat-visit businesses. If you own a Michigan business with a story worth telling, or you represent a bank, credit union, insurer, chamber, or other community-minded organization looking for a more meaningful way to support local businesses, let's connect.

Give the profile an immediate job.

New banner direction

Michigan businesses should understand the promise before they read the profile.
